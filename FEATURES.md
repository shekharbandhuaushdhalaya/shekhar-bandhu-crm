# Shekhar Bandhu CRM - Comprehensive Feature List

## 1. Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- **Contact & Lead Management:** Track contacts, companies, leads, and deal values. Log calls, emails, and meetings.
- **Sales Targets & Incentives:** Set and track targets for sales representatives, calculate incentives and bonuses automatically.
- **Medical Representatives (MR):** Manage MR hierarchies, assign territories, track daily field intelligence, sample bag inventories, and sample issuances via OTP.
- **Doctors & Parties:** Specialized profiles for managing interactions, pricing tiers, loyalty schemes, and prescriptions for doctors and vendors/customers.
- **Automated Workflows:** Drip campaigns and lead nurturing via automated rules and email triggers.

## 2. Sales & Orders
- **Quotations & Estimates:** Generate professional quotations, convert them seamlessly to orders.
- **Sales Orders:** Track end-to-end sales orders, apply flexible customer pricing, tiered discounts, and handle tax logic (HSN, GST).
- **Challans (Delivery Notes):** Issue delivery challans before final invoices.
- **Invoicing:** Generate Sale and Purchase Invoices, including Recurring Subscriptions/Invoices.
- **Returns & Credit Notes:** Manage sales returns, issue credit notes and debit notes automatically tied to original invoices.

## 3. Financial & Accounting
- **Payments & Receipts:** Track incoming and outgoing payments against invoices.
- **Bank Reconciliation:** Match system ledger entries with bank statement uploads.
- **GST Returns Data Export:** Generate GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B compliant reports.
- **E-way Bills Integration:** Direct e-way bill generation for dispatches over threshold values.
- **Payment Gateway Integration:** Collect payments via online links for outstanding invoices.

## 4. Inventory & Warehousing
- **Multi-Warehouse Management:** Track stock across manufacturing units, quarantine areas, and finished goods warehouses.
- **Barcode & QR Scanning:** Scan products for rapid stocktakes and dispatch verification.
- **Stock Movements:** Log stock transfers, raw material entries, and generate stock ledgers.
- **Reorder & Low Stock Alerts:** Automated warnings for low stock based on historical demand and set minimums.
- **Expiry Tracking:** Advanced alerts for near-expiry finished goods and raw materials.

## 5. Manufacturing & AYUSH Compliance
- **Batch Manufacturing Records (BMR):** Track end-to-end batch production, yield, and phase clearances.
- **Bill of Materials (BOM):** Multi-level recipes mapping raw materials to finished products.
- **Quality Assurance & Control (QA/QC):**
  - Product-specific AYUSH QC specifications with approval workflows.
  - Certificate of Analysis (CoA) generation strictly driven by specifications.
  - Mandatory dual electronic signatures (Chemist + QA) for batch release.
  - Strict release gates blocking unapproved batches from market dispatch.
- **Pharmacopoeia Dictionary:** Integrated Ayurvedic Pharmacopoeia (API) monograph dictionary for auto-validating botanical and scientific names.
- **GMP Compliance Dashboard:** Centralized tracking for equipment calibration, deviations, CAPA, product recalls, vendor qualifications, and stability studies.

## 6. Analytics & Intelligence
- **AI Analytics & Reporting:** Digestible insights, demand forecasting, and predictive analysis.
- **Dashboards:** Real-time data visualization of revenue, order volumes, rep performance, and inventory health.
- **Report Digests:** Automated scheduled reports sent via email.

## 7. Operational & System Features
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Granular permissions for admins, managers, QA staff, and field reps.
- **Audit Logging:** System-wide trace logs for tracking user actions and modifications.
- **In-App Notifications:** Real-time bell notifications and event-driven alerts.
- **Data Export/Import:** Comprehensive CSV/Excel import and export capabilities.
